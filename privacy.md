# Privacy Policy for Health Supervisor

**Last Updated:** 26 September 2026

This privacy policy applies to the **Health Supervisor** app (Arabic: استدعاءات التطعيم) for mobile devices, operated by **Montaser Mansour** (the "Service Provider").

---

## Summary

**Health Supervisor does NOT collect, transmit, or share any personal data with any server.**

All data entered into the app (child information, vaccination records, contact history, notes) is stored **locally on your device only** using a private SQLite database.

---

## Information We Do NOT Collect

We do **NOT** collect:

- Personal information (name, email, phone number, address)
- Device information (IP address, device ID, operating system)
- Usage analytics (pages visited, time spent, actions)
- Location data
- Cookies or tracking identifiers
- Advertising identifiers

---

## Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The application stores the following data **locally** on your device:

- **Children records:** Names, birth dates, contact numbers, vaccination records
- **Vaccination schedules:** Imported from CSV files you provide
- **Contact history:** Records of calls, SMS, and WhatsApp messages you send
- **Notes:** Personal notes you add about each child
- **Settings:** Office name, governorate, custom messages, session days
- **Backups:** Only when you choose to export a backup file

**This data is never transmitted to us or any third party.**

You can delete this data at any time by:
- Using the app's built-in delete options
- Clearing the app's data from your device settings
- Uninstalling the application

---

## Permissions Used

The application requests the following Android permissions:

### 1. **Internet Access**
- **Purpose:** Check for app updates and download new versions from GitHub
- **Data transmitted:** None (only reads a public JSON file)

### 2. **Network State**
- **Purpose:** Detect whether an internet connection is available
- **Data transmitted:** None

### 3. **Read Media / Storage (optional)**
- **Purpose:** Import CSV files you select
- **Data transmitted:** None (files are read locally only)

### 4. **Install Packages (REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES)**
- **Purpose:** Allow self-update of the app via downloaded APK
- **Data transmitted:** None

---

## Third-Party Services

The application interacts with the following third-party services **only when you choose to**:

### 1. GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com)
- **When:** On app launch, to check for updates
- **Purpose:** Read a public JSON file to see if a new version is available
- **Data shared:** None — GitHub only receives a standard HTTP request (anonymous)
- **Privacy Policy:** https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies

### 2. WhatsApp
- **When:** Only when you tap the "Send WhatsApp" button
- **Purpose:** Open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message
- **Data shared:** The message and the recipient's phone number (as you provide)
- **Privacy Policy:** https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy

### 3. Google Play Services (if installed via Google Play)
- **When:** App installation and updates through Google Play
- **Purpose:** Distribution and automatic updates
- **Privacy Policy:** https://policies.google.com/privacy

**We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.**

---

## How the App Uses Your Data

All data is processed **entirely on your device** for the following purposes:

1. **Display children lists** (missed / newborns)
2. **Calculate vaccination schedules** based on birth dates
3. **Generate reminder messages** for parents
4. **Track your communication history** with each child
5. **Export reports** as CSV files (stored locally)

**No processing occurs on any external server.**

---

## Data Sharing

We do **NOT** share your data with anyone.

Your data is only shared when **you explicitly choose to**:
- Send a WhatsApp message to a parent
- Export a CSV report to send manually
- Create a backup file to move to another device

---

## Data Retention

Data is retained **on your device** until:
- You delete it manually within the app
- You clear app data from device settings
- You uninstall the application

**We have no access to your data and cannot delete it remotely.**

---

## Security

We take the following measures to protect your data:

- **Local storage only:** Data stays on your device
- **No external servers:** Nothing is transmitted to the internet
- **No tracking:** No analytics or identifiers
- **Signed updates:** App updates are signed with a private key

**Note:** Android's app sandbox prevents other apps from accessing your data without root access.

---

## Children's Privacy

The application is designed for use by **health supervisors and community health workers** as part of their professional duties.

The app handles children's data (names, birth dates, vaccination records) under the supervision of the health worker. This data is:
- Stored locally on the health worker's device
- Never transmitted to us or any third party
- Protected by Android's app sandbox

We do **not** knowingly collect personal information from children directly. Children under 16 should not use this app on their own.

---

## Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store any data on our servers, there is **no data to access, correct, or delete from us**.

To manage your data:
- **Delete individual records** from within the app
- **Export backups** at any time
- **Uninstall** the app to delete all data
